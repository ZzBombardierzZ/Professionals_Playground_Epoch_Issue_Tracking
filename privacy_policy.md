# Privacy Policy — Professionals Playground

**Last updated: May 1, 2026**

---

## Section 1 — Who We Are

**Professionals Playground** operates a private ArmA 2 / DayZ Epoch game server and an associated Discord community. The services described in this policy are operated by **Bomb (Jeremiah)**.

For privacy inquiries, contact: [data@bomb99.dev](mailto:data@bomb99.dev)

Collection of game account identifiers, IP addresses, player names, session data, and communications is standard practice across virtually all game servers. Most servers do not disclose this publicly. We publish this policy because we want you to know what we collect, why we keep it, who can access it, and what we will never do with it.

---

## Section 2 — What Data We Collect and Why

### 2.1 Identity Data

- **Collected:** Game account identifiers (e.g. Steam UID), Discord account identifiers and profile information
- **Purpose:** Link your game account to your Discord account; display your identity on community features such as leaderboards; enforce community rules and moderation actions
- **Legal basis:** Legitimate interest (community administration); Consent (voluntary account linking)

### 2.2 Gameplay Data

- **Collected:** In-game character name and activity history; gameplay statistics and performance records; in-game asset ownership and associated state; community program participation
- **Purpose:** Operate community features such as leaderboards, statistics, and player-facing tools; support community programs
- **Retention:** Per-round gameplay data is cleared on server wipe. Some historical records may be retained across wipes.

### 2.3 Transaction & Economy Data

- **Collected:** In-game currency balances and transaction history; purchase records and related activity; referral and reward records; administrative transaction records
- **Purpose:** Operate the in-game economy; prevent fraud and support dispute resolution
- **Retention:** Economy and transaction data persists across wipes and may be retained for the lifespan of the community.

### 2.4 Network & Connection Data

- **Collected:** IP addresses associated with game server connections and web panel access; connection patterns used for security analysis
- **Purpose:** Detect and prevent ban evasion, multi-accounting, and abuse; server security and moderation
- **Retention:** Retained indefinitely for moderation purposes.
- **Note:** IP addresses are not sold or shared with third parties. They are accessible only to the server administrator.

### 2.5 Communications Data

- **Collected:** Messages and content submitted to community tools (e.g. support tickets, bot commands, Discord interactions); Discord activity indicators (e.g. voice session presence, server join/leave)
- **Purpose:** Operate the support system; community engagement tracking; moderation
- **Retention:** Persists across wipes and may be retained for the lifespan of the community.

### 2.6 Moderation Data

- **Collected:** Records of moderation actions taken within the community; administrator activity logs for accountability; flagged player records and associated notes
- **Purpose:** Maintain a safe and fair community environment; ensure accountability for administrative actions
- **Retention:** Retained indefinitely. Necessary for consistent moderation across server rounds.

### 2.7 Technical Data

- **Collected:** Aggregated server performance and health metrics; anonymised or aggregate player count data; game server log data used for anti-cheat and balance analysis
- **Purpose:** Server administration and performance monitoring; exploit detection and game balance
- **Retention:** Most technical log data is cleared on server wipe.

## Section 3 — How We Share Your Data

We do **not** sell your data. We do **not** share your personal data with external parties for advertising, analytics, or any commercial purpose.

Your data may be accessible to:

- The server administrator (Bomb/Jeremiah), for moderation and community management
- Other community members — only information you have made visible through participation in public features (e.g. leaderboards)
- BattleMetrics — a third-party server monitoring and moderation platform (see Section 6)
- Our hosting provider — server infrastructure and storage

We may disclose data if required by law or to prevent serious harm.

**We will never:**

- Sell your data
- Share your data with advertisers or data brokers
- Use your data to target you with advertising
- Share your personal data with other gaming communities without your consent, except where required by law or to prevent serious harm

---

## Section 4 — Data Retention

Some data is cleared each time the game server wipes (new round):

- Per-round character and gameplay statistics
- In-game activity logs tied to the current round
- Temporary game state data

Other data is retained across wipes and may be stored for multiple years — potentially for the entire lifespan of this community:

- Identity links between game and Discord accounts
- Economy and transaction history
- IP address and connection records
- Communications and support records
- Moderation and audit records
- Community program participation history

We do not currently enforce automatic deletion timelines on retained data. BattleMetrics data retention is governed by their own policies and is outside our control once transmitted to their platform.

You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at [data@bomb99.dev](mailto:data@bomb99.dev). Some records may be retained where there is a legitimate moderation interest (e.g. an active ban). We will let you know if we are unable to fulfil a deletion request and explain why.

## Section 5 — Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

- **Access** — request a copy of the data we hold about you
- **Rectification** — ask us to correct inaccurate data
- **Erasure** — request deletion of your data, subject to legitimate moderation interest exceptions
- **Restriction** — ask us to limit how we process certain data
- **Portability** — receive your data in a portable format
- **Objection** — object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise any of these rights, contact: [data@bomb99.dev](mailto:data@bomb99.dev). We aim to respond within 30 days.

## Section 6 — Third-Party Services

- **BattleMetrics** — We use BattleMetrics for server monitoring and community moderation. When you connect to our game server, BattleMetrics independently collects data including player names, IP addresses, Steam account information, in-game communications, and session history. This data is subject to BattleMetrics' own privacy policy: [battlemetrics.com/privacy](https://www.battlemetrics.com/privacy). Some information (such as player names and session history) may be publicly searchable through their platform. We do not control what BattleMetrics stores, displays, or retains. To request removal of your data from BattleMetrics, contact them directly via their policy page.
- **Discord** — Account identifiers, usernames, and message interactions are subject to Discord's Privacy Policy: [discord.com/privacy](https://discord.com/privacy)
- **Steam** — Steam account identifiers are public by nature. See Valve's Privacy Policy: [store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/)
- **Hosting Provider** — Server infrastructure and data storage are provided by our hosting provider. See their own data processing terms.
